Questions

Why not use an automated tool?

Use one for filing if you like. A tool answers disputes after they happen. This work reduces how many you get and decides which ones are worth fighting.

Can you guarantee a win rate?

No. The cardholder's bank decides every dispute. I can promise a complete response sent before the deadline, and straight advice on the cases you should accept.

Which processors do you cover?

Disputes on any of them, because the evidence rules come from the card networks. Fraud rules depend on what your processor lets you change. Stripe gives the most control.

I only get a few disputes a month. Is it worth it?

If they are small and rare, probably not, and I will tell you that on the free call. It pays off when disputes are growing, order values are high, or your provider has warned you.
